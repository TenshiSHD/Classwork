Agile Phases

Agile Phase Description
Concept the initial stage of a project where the project's purpose, scope, and feasibility are defined, often using language-independent tools like pseudocode
Inception The project vision is defined, and high-level requirements are gathered through collaboration with stakeholders.
Iteration a cyclical process of repeatedly building, testing, and refining a product
Release the stage of the software development life cycle (SDLC) where a completed and tested product is transitioned from development to live operation and made available to users
Maintenance the final stage in the software development lifecycle where a deployed product is supported with updates and fixes
Retirement the final stage in the software development life cycle
